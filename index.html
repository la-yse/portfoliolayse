ABOUT ME

I'm a Front-End Developer based in Lisbon, Portugal. My goal is to build solid and functional websites that put user experience first.

I believe every project deserves the right tools, not the most tools, so I develop solutions around each project's needs, keeping them efficient, focused, and maintainable.

As the web becomes increasingly automated, I believe individuality still matters.

Websites made by humans, for humans.
